    moved -0.39 points over the 10-day gap.

  THIS IS A FINDING ABOUT THE SAMPLING CADENCE, NOT ABOUT NIFTY.
  sigma_atm_prev goes stale at exactly the rate the sample is
  thinned. With weekly files the benchmark is a week old, and the
  volatility LEVEL move over that week swamps the smile shape -
  14% against 86% here. Master plan 5.5 asks the model to
  learn 'only the systematic part of the model error'. A level
  move over a week is the UNsystematic part: it is roughly a
  random walk, and no function of [m, T, r] can predict it.

  sigma_hist does not have this problem. It is computed from the
  daily NIFTY close series, which is complete regardless of how
  often the option files are sampled. That makes the week1.py
  re-run a design requirement rather than a missing column.

CHECKPOINT
==============================================================================
  Does residual learning reduce real-market Black-Scholes error?

  ON SYNTHETIC DATA, DEFINITIVELY YES. Where the model error is
  known by construction, the best hybrid cuts RMSE from 1.4218 to
  0.0572, a factor of 24.9, and beats the best pure model (0.1635)
  with the same learners. Black-Scholes supplies the structure;
  the model supplies only what the formula misses.

  ON REAL DATA, ALSO YES - out of sample, on dates the models
  never saw. Black-Scholes 35.00, best hybrid 27.33 (Random forest, hybrid):
  a 22% reduction in RMSE on 2295 quotes over 4 held-out dates.
  The hybrid beats Black-Scholes in 4 of 5 moneyness buckets and
  4 of 5 maturity buckets, so the gain is not one region.

  PURE MACHINE LEARNING FAILS ON THE SAME DATA: the best pure
  model reaches only 116.9 against the benchmark's 35.00. Without
  Black-Scholes to supply the structure, 6,443 training quotes
  are nowhere near enough to learn a pricing surface.

  AND THE RANKING INVERTS FROM THE SYNTHETIC CASE. The network
  was the best hybrid on synthetic data (0.057) and is the worst
  here (150.9); the forest and even OLS generalise better. On a
  noiseless target capacity wins; on 12 dates of noisy market
  quotes it overfits. That is the clearest argument in the whole
  project for validating on the data you actually have.

  On the residual itself: daily files cut the stale-volatility
  component from 63% to 14%, exactly as intended, leaving
  smile shape as 86% of what the hybrid has to learn.
